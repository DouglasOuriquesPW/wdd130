Our mission is to deliver safe, thrilling, and memorable white water rafting experiences guided by trained professionals.

History

While our company was officially founded in 1998, our story began many years earlier. Driven by a deep love for the water and extreme sports, the Frog family spent years exploring and conquering rivers across the region. As more people in town began noticing our expeditions and asking to join, we saw the potential to turn our family passion into a dedicated rafting outfitter.
